Agriculture employs more people in Uttar Pradesh than any other sector, and it
hires graduates who understand both the science and the business. **B.Sc
Agriculture** is a four-year professional degree, affiliated at City College of
Management to the University of Lucknow, that prepares students for exactly
that work.

## What you study

The syllabus covers agronomy, soil science, horticulture, plant breeding and
genetics, plant pathology, entomology, agricultural economics, extension
education and farm machinery. Alongside this are practical sessions [on the
college's agricultural field / at partner farms — confirm], crop production
exercises and rural awareness work. The final year includes experiential
learning and rural work experience [as per the current University of Lucknow
syllabus].

The practical half matters. Employers in this sector interview for field
judgement — can you read a soil test, identify a pest, advise a farmer — and
that is learnt outdoors, not from notes.

## Where graduates work

**Government and public sector.** Agriculture Development Officer, extension
officer, and roles filled through UPPSC, UPSSSC and similar examinations.
Several central recruitments — IBPS Agriculture Field Officer, NABARD, FCI —
specifically ask for an agriculture degree.

**Agri-business companies.** Field officer and territory roles with seed,
fertiliser, agrochemical, irrigation and food-processing companies, which
recruit heavily across eastern and central UP.

**Banking and insurance.** Rural banking roles and agriculture loan appraisal,
where an agriculture degree is a stated preference, and crop insurance
assessment.

**Your own enterprise.** Nursery and seed supply, protected cultivation, soil
testing services, farm advisory, and food processing. Agri-entrepreneurship is
a realistic route in this region, not a theoretical one.

## Government exam preparation

If a government post is your aim, start in the second year rather than after
graduation: the syllabus for most agriculture posts overlaps with your degree
subjects, and the advantage goes to candidates who revise alongside the course.
Faculty at CCM guide students preparing for government agriculture examinations.

## Higher study

M.Sc Agriculture in a specialisation such as agronomy, horticulture, soil
science or plant protection is the usual next step, and is required for most
research and teaching posts. An MBA in agri-business suits students who find
they prefer the commercial side.
