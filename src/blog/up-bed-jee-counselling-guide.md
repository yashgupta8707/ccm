Every year, graduates in Lucknow ask the same question: *how do I get admission
to B.Ed at a particular college?* In Uttar Pradesh the answer is the same for
every NCTE-recognised institution, including City College of Management. Seats
are allotted through the **Uttar Pradesh B.Ed Joint Entrance Examination (UP
B.Ed JEE)** and its online counselling, not by applying to the college.

## Who is eligible

A bachelor's or master's degree with at least 50% marks, with relaxation for
reserved categories as per rules, in line with NCTE and state norms [confirm
current criteria]. Final-year students should check whether the current
notification allows them to appear [confirm].

## The sequence, step by step

1. **Watch for the notification.** The examining university publishes the UP
   B.Ed JEE notification and online application form [confirm dates for 2027].
2. **Apply and pay the fee** within the dates given, and download your admit
   card when it is released.
3. **Sit the examination.** It has two papers — general knowledge and language,
   then general aptitude and your subject ability [confirm the current pattern].
4. **Check your rank.** The result gives you a state rank, which decides when
   your counselling round opens.
5. **Register for counselling** and pay the counselling fee in the window given
   for your rank.
6. **Fill your choices.** This is the important step: search for *City College
   of Management* and add it using college code **[college code]**. Put the
   colleges you actually want above the ones you do not.
7. **Accept the allotment,** download the allotment letter, and pay any seat
   acceptance fee within the deadline.
8. **Report to the college** with the allotment letter and your documents for
   verification and fee payment.

## Documents to carry when you report

Class 10 and 12 marksheets and certificates, graduation marksheets and degree,
UP B.Ed JEE admit card, rank card and allotment letter, transfer and character
certificates, migration certificate where applicable, caste, income and
domicile certificates where applicable, Aadhaar card, and passport-size
photographs. The checklist is on our [Admissions page](/admissions/#documents).

## How to judge a B.Ed college

Rank and fees are easy to compare. These matter more over two years:

- **Recognition.** Confirm the college is recognised by NCTE for B.Ed, and ask
  which body it is affiliated to. At CCM, B.Ed is NCTE recognised; the
  affiliating body is [University of Lucknow — confirm].
- **School internship.** A B.Ed is judged by classroom time. Ask which partner
  schools the college places trainees in, and who mentors them.
- **Labs and resources.** Psychology, ICT and teaching-aid facilities [confirm].
- **Eligibility-test support.** Ask whether faculty prepare students for CTET
  and UPTET, which you will need for government school jobs.
- **Distance.** Two years of travel is a real cost. CCM is on Faizabad Road in
  Chinhat, near Crown Mall.
