City College of Management, Tiwariganj, Faizabad Road, Chinhat, has opened
admissions for the **2027-28** session in all seven programmes.

## Programmes open for admission

| Programme | Duration | Eligibility | Affiliation / recognition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BBA | 3 years | 10+2 in any stream | University of Lucknow |
| BCA | 3 years | 10+2 in any stream | University of Lucknow |
| B.Com | 3 years | 10+2 in any stream | University of Lucknow |
| B.Sc Agriculture | 4 years | 10+2 with Biology / Agriculture [confirm] | University of Lucknow |
| B.Ed | 2 years | Graduation or post-graduation as per NCTE norms | NCTE recognised |
| M.Ed | 2 years | B.Ed with minimum marks as per NCTE norms | NCTE recognised |
| D.El.Ed | 2 years | Graduation as per state norms | NCTE recognised |

## Two admission routes

**BBA, BCA, B.Com and B.Sc Agriculture** are admitted directly by the college as
per University of Lucknow norms [confirm]. Fill in the form on the
[Admissions page](/admissions/#apply) and a counsellor will call you within one
working day.

**B.Ed, M.Ed and D.El.Ed** are admitted through the state processes. B.Ed
candidates appear in the UP B.Ed JEE and choose the college during online
counselling using college code [college code]. D.El.Ed candidates apply through
the state's online application and merit-based counselling.
